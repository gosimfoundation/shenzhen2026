## How to Submit

1. Download and fill out the reimbursement template above.
2. Scan or photograph all receipts (must show date, item, and amount paid). Zip all receipt files into one folder, rename to your full name.
3. Email the completed template and zipped receipts to [travel@gosim.org](mailto:travel@gosim.org).

**Email title format**: `GOSIM Shenzhen 2026 Reimbursement: [Speaker/Spotlight] [Your Name] - [Total Amount in CNY] - [Departure City]`<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Example: `GOSIM Shenzhen 2026 Reimbursement: Speaker Jane Smith - ¥8,500 - Tokyo`

**Deadline**: Submit by **December 1, 2026** (45 days after the event). Reimbursements are processed within 45 business days after submission and attendance verification.

---

## Eligibility

Travel funding is limited to **Confirmed Speakers** and **Spotlight Project Members** (max one reimbursement quota per Spotlight project).

Reimbursement caps are based on travel distance to Shenzhen:

- **Tier 1 — Intercontinental**: Travel from outside Asia (North America, South America, Europe, Africa, Oceania).
- **Tier 2 — Regional**: Travel from within Asia (Japan, South Korea, Singapore, India, Thailand, etc.).
- Travel within Shenzhen is not reimbursed.

Your specific cap will be communicated in your acceptance email.

## Covered Expenses

| Category | Covered | Not Covered |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| **Airfare** | Economy round-trip to Shenzhen. Itinerary must show cost and payment method. | Upgrades, seat selection, baggage fees, travel insurance |
| **Accommodation** | October 15–17, 2026 (event dates + 1 night prior). Room rate and tax only. | Meals, mini-bar, phone charges, dry cleaning, bottled water |
| **Ground Transport** | Not covered | All local transport in Shenzhen |

**Also not covered**: Visas · Meals · Daily commuting to Aden Hotel Shenzhen Nanshan · Rental cars · Luxury rides · Conversion/bank fees · Personal travel days

## General Rules

- **Event attendance is required.** Provide proof of attendance (e.g., a photo of your talk) to be reimbursed.
- Book refundable travel when possible. Unused non-refundable travel will not be reimbursed.
- GOSIM reserves the right to request additional documentation.
- Policy violations (fake receipts, incomplete attendance, overlapping event claims) result in a permanent ban from GOSIM travel funding.

For questions, contact [travel@gosim.org](mailto:travel@gosim.org).
